IT / OT architecture · RCA agents

From plant signal to operator action

  1. OT and plant systems (Purdue L0–2)DCS through its OPC server, historian (back-fill and benchmarks), lab data (LIMS or lab sheets: coal, water, flue gas), maintenance (CMMS work orders).
  2. I/O Connect in the DMZ (L3.5)OPC UA / DA, SQL and REST clients. Read-only by default, outbound-only connections, a local buffer with back-fill, units, scaling and timestamps aligned, and tags mapped to ontology objects.
  3. IT, on-prem or cloud (L4–5)UDF holds time-series, KPIs, design bands and golden-batch envelopes. The Ontology and Smart context hold plant knowledge (documents are uploaded here on the IT side). Bruce runs the agents. Models come from Deepsense (local) or cloud APIs.
  4. People and outputsI/O Sense RCA dashboard, daily summary for the plant head and shift in-charge, and one-click operator feedback. Optional push to an existing APM or work-order system through API or MCP.
Agents never write to the control system. They read through governed tools and hand their findings to people. Forge deploys on plant servers, private cloud, hybrid or air-gapped. Foundry scopes access and keeps the audit trail.
Use case 01 · Power plant co-pilot

Deviations are easy to see, hard to explain

Site: pulp & paper mill · 21 MW cogeneration plant with a coal-fired AFBC boiler, covering boiler, turbine and water chemistry.

The DCS shows when a value drifts out of band. It doesn't say why, or what the shift should change.

  • Slow drifts in heat transfer, air ingress and steam conditions are found late, after efficiency or load is already lost.
  • One symptom has many causes, and working back to the real one takes trend digging.
  • Diagnosis depends on the most experienced person on shift. Maintenance and process issues sit in separate silos.

Use case 01 · Agent orchestration

Four agents, each with one job and its own tools

Each agent is built in Bruce with its own model, skills (versioned RCA procedures), instructions, tool list and the agents it may call. Agents only see the slice of smart context they need. Every step is traced. Click an agent:

1 · Trend and statisticsStatisticalDeterministic

Job: computes statistics for every monitored parameter over the rolling hourly window.

Tools: time-series and KPI connectors; data-quality checks for stale, frozen or out-of-range tags.

Hands over: level, direction, rate of change, time spent out of band.

2 · Benchmark and deviationStatisticalRules

Job: compares the statistics with HMBD and OEM design values, and with golden-batch data for the current load factor, coal type and season. Raises an issue when a limit is crossed.

Tools: HMBD design values, design bands, UCL / LCL limits, golden-batch envelopes in UDF.

Hands over: deviation record: parameter, band, duration, severity.

3 · Root cause analysisKnowledge graphLLM

Job: follows ontology relationships to every related KPI and parameter, compares each one, and works out why the issue happened.

Tools: ontology traversal; smart context search (manuals, maintenance history, operating characteristics); RCA skill; LLM reasoning.

Hands over: problem statement, probable cause and the evidence behind it.

4 · Action and savingLLMCalculation

Job: turns the RCA into a specific, plain-language action, and estimates the probable net heat rate saving against HMBD design values.

Tools: OEM and SOP passages from smart context; HMBD design values and KPI calculation tools.

Hands over: RCA card on the I/O Sense dashboard and the daily summary.

Schedule (hourly) or alarm → Trend → Deviation → RCA → Action and saving → Dashboard and daily summary → Operator rating

Use case 02 · Auxiliary power reduction

A large cost that few plants can trace

Site: zinc & silver producer (Hindustan Zinc) · 90 MW captive power plant built to supply its own smelters · Application live savings validation under way

Fans, pumps, mills and cooling towers draw almost a tenth of what a captive plant generates. Most plants can report the total, but not which unit, condition or shift caused it.

  • Daily auxiliary consumption (APC) ran at 7.4–8.3% of generation in October 2026, about 6.5 MW.
  • Losses showed up in the next morning's spreadsheet, after the shift that caused them had ended.
  • Load swings driven by the power exchange (IEX) make fixed running rules a poor fit.

Use case 02 · Agent orchestration

The same four-agent chain, tuned for kilowatts

Built in Bruce on the same ontology and smart context as the RCA co-pilot, so boiler, turbine and auxiliary findings can be read together. Every kW figure comes from calculations, curves or benchmarks. The LLM explains and recommends.

1 · Signal health and statisticsDeterministicStatistical

Job: checks the 114 drive signals are live, then computes kW, kWh and APC per drive and system over the hour and the plant day.

Tools: time-series and meter connectors; continuity, stale, flatline and out-of-span checks.

2 · Benchmark and deviationStatisticalRules

Job: compares each drive with what it should draw: the plant's best day at the same load, season and coal type, the unit's own performance curve, and HMBD / OEM design values.

Hands over: excess kW per drive and system, with the reference it was judged against.

3 · Root cause analysisKnowledge graphLLM

Job: follows ontology links from each excess to the valves, pressures, gas temperatures and related drives that explain it.

Tools: ontology traversal; smart context search (HMBD, OEM curves, manuals, maintenance); RCA skill.

4 · Action and savingLLMCalculation

Job: turns each finding into a safe, stepwise action with stop conditions, and estimates the kW it can save using curve and calculation tools.

Hands over: recommendation cards, the daily summary and the day's loss ranking.

Planning tools alongside the agents

  • Day-ahead scheduler: from the generation plan and each unit's curve, picks which BFP and FD / PA / ID fans run in every 15-minute slot (up to 7 days ahead). A second view plans 6 CT fans and 4 mills against actual.
  • Coal benchmarking: past days are clustered into 4 coal personas (premium, standard, wet, poor) from GCV, moisture, volatiles, ash and fixed carbon, which sets the expected APC for today's coal.
  • Performance reports: boiler efficiency (heat-loss method), turbine heat rate and specific coal consumption, with losses split into controllable and uncontrollable.

Use case 03 · Soft sensor

Estimating soot that no sensor can measure

Deposit is treated as a hidden state of each zone, observed through its thermal signature: a fouled surface absorbs less heat, so its exit gas runs hotter and draft loss rises.

Flue-gas temp at each of the 7 zonesHeat absorbed by each surface; a rise across a zone signals fouling
ID-fan speed, furnace draftGas-side resistance; draft loss grows with deposit
Main steam temp and flowSteam-side heat uptake, and the load the zones are working at
Soot blower status flagsWhich surfaces were just cleaned, to see the response

Models and algorithms

ComponentTypeRunsWhat it does
Expectation–Maximization (EM)System identificationOnce, offlineLearns the per-zone grey-box fouling model's parameters from months of this boiler's own DCS history
Extended Kalman FilterState estimationLive, ~5 minFuses the 15 signals into a 0–100% deposit per zone, with uncertainty. This is the soft sensor.
Receding-horizon optimiserOptimisation (MPC-style)Every cycle; batches ~2 hRanks blowers and plans batches to hold thermal and draft limits with the least steam
Relief and priority rulesDeterministicEvery cycleCorner blowers first, economiser and boiler-bank relief, no-starve rotation
Interlock gateDeterministicEvery few seconds9 conditions, all must hold before any blow
Answer to the meeting question: yes, a Kalman filter, specifically an Extended Kalman Filter running on a model learned from plant data with EM. That is classical machine learning (system identification) plus estimation and optimisation. No neural network and no LLM in the control loop. Built and served on Deepsense. Model structure, parameters and tuning are Faclon proprietary.

The 3D chart runs a real 1-D Kalman filter on simulated data to show how the estimate and its uncertainty band behave. It is not plant data.

Use case 03 · Decision logic

Blow only where it pays

Blowing is triggered by estimated deposit and process limits, never by a timer. Steam goes where heat recovered per blow is highest, and that is where the saving comes from.

Batch rules

  • Each blower takes the deposit of the dirtiest zone it reaches. The top 8 form one batch (up to 10 on boiler-bank relief), roughly every 2 h.
  • Cap of 100 blows per operating day (corner blowers exempt). ~0.66 t steam per blow; daily target ~60–70 t.
  • Overrides: economiser outlet > 182 °C forces economiser blowers; boiler-bank inlet > 600 °C forces relief; 9 key lances at least once every 3 batches; any blower idle 3 batches is forced in.
  • ID-fan protection: normal blowers fire only with both fans < 930 RPM; corner blowers fire in 3 stages if RPM stays high; still high → treated as a load problem, batch stops, operator alerted.
  • Header charging: charged only around the blowing window (drain > 250 °C). Not charged in 30 min → skip, retry in 10. Closed once all lances are home.
Use case 03 · Safety

Nine checks before every blow

All nine must hold at the same time. A blow is held, never forced. Temperature and pressure checks need the last 5 readings to pass, not an average.

Use case 03 · OT security architecture

Two firewalls, one bounded write path

The control network is untouched. Everything Faclon runs sits between two firewalls, and only one bounded component can write to the process.

  • South firewall: no inbound sessions into OT, no default route north.
  • I/O Connect (collector and historian): OPC UA client to the DCS's existing OPC UA server, with a local store-and-forward buffer. OPC UA stops here and MQTT starts here. Publishes only chosen values north, outbound only.
  • APC engine in its own zone: runs the soft sensor and scheduler, reads tags and writes blow commands. Its account is scoped to its own tags, and it has no route north at all. The controller clamps writes for rate and range.
  • Jump host: second, separate authentication with every session recorded. It is the only path to tune the APC.
  • North firewall: one inbound rule, outbound MQTT only. Remote access goes through IT-owned VPN, named accounts and MFA.
  • IT / cloud: UDF, Deepsense and I/O Sense dashboards; lab entry through a web form.

Why a boiler-side loop matters to a turbine-focused portfolio

  • More steam to the turbine: steam no longer spent on unneeded blows, plus the SGR gain.
  • Steadier inlet conditions: narrower main steam temperature band, and every blow is held if pressure drops below 63 kg/cm².
  • Lower auxiliary load: ID-fan speed down 20–80 RPM, which links to use case 02.
  • Reusable pattern: hidden-state estimation from existing thermal signals also fits condensers and heat exchangers (candidates, not deployments).
Bringing it together

Two ways AI acts on a plant

Advisory agents

Read only. Statistics, ontology and LLM reasoning produce a cause and an action, and a person decides. Used in 01 RCA and 02 aux power.

Closed-loop control

Estimation and optimisation (EKF + MPC), no LLM. Writes bounded commands through a 9-point gate, with DCS confirmation. Used in 03 soot blowing.

How it plugs into GE Vernova's ecosystem

  • Reads what is already there: historians, OPC servers and APM alerts as sources and triggers. No new sensors.
  • Writes back where people work: RCAs and actions go into the existing APM, case-management or work-order system through APIs or MCP.
  • Open agent interfaces: the Bruce MCP Gateway lets other agentic tools call these agents, and lets the agents call other models, agents and MCP servers.
  • Model choice: cloud APIs or local and sovereign LLMs. An existing anomaly or performance model can feed the detection agents.
  • Deploy anywhere: plant servers, cloud, hybrid or air-gapped, with the DMZ pattern and traced, scoped access.

Booth and deep-dive preparation

Q&A readiness

What exactly makes these "agents"?

Four objective-scoped agents on Bruce, each with its own model, versioned skills, instructions, a scoped tool list (via the MCP Gateway) and a smart-context slice. They hand structured outputs to each other, and every tool call and retrieval is traced. Ready

Which models? Is the LLM making up numbers?

RCA: rules, statistics, golden-batch envelopes, ontology traversal, semantic retrieval, and an LLM only for reasoning and text (Claude / ChatGPT API or a local LLM on Deepsense). Numbers such as KPIs and NHR savings come from calculation tools. Every alert traces to a stated limit. Ready Which LLM runs at the site TBC

Is a Kalman filter really ML?

The soot-blowing soft sensor is a grey-box state-space model whose parameters are learned from plant data with EM (system identification). An Extended Kalman Filter then estimates deposit live, with uncertainty, and an MPC-style optimiser schedules blows. That is classical ML plus estimation and control: no deep learning, no LLM. Ready

What data points were analysed?

RCA: 37 named parameters hourly plus 22 KPIs, lab, load / coal / season and maintenance history. Aux: 114 drive signals in 7 families plus generation plan and coal / water lab. Soot: 15 named DCS signals plus interlock signals. Ready

Do you write to our control system?

RCA and aux agents: never, read-only. Soot blowing: one bounded write path from an APC engine in its own zone, clamped by the controller, behind a 9-point gate, confirmed by the DCS. Ready

Can it run on-prem or air-gapped? Where does data go?

Yes. Forge deploys on plant servers, private cloud, hybrid or air-gapped. Deepsense serves local / sovereign LLMs so prompts and data stay on site. I/O Connect is outbound-only from the DMZ. Ready

How is 98.4% measured?

It is the validated accuracy of agent recommendations in live operation. Operators rate every RCA with one click, and ratings are stored in UDF. Exact validation method and sample size TBC

Do you compete with GE Vernova's platforms?

No. It is a capability that plugs in: it reads existing historians and APM, writes into existing work-order systems, and exposes agents over MCP. Ready
